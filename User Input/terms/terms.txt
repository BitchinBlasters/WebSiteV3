## 11. Gel blaster use and safety
Gel blasters are replicas that can look like real firearms, and police may treat them that way. Use yours responsibly and follow the law and the rules of the field or venue you play at.
- Wear full-seal eye protection whenever a blaster is in use, and never point one at anyone who isn't wearing it.
- Use blasters only on private property with permission or at approved venues, and follow their power (FPS) and age rules.
- Carry your blaster covered and out of sight in public, and store it securely, unloaded and away from children.
- You must be 18 or older to order a blaster, and we may ask for proof of age.
- Never modify a blaster to fire anything other than gel balls.
- Always disconnect, handle, charge and store batteries correctly, following the manufacturer's instructions, and never leave a battery charging unattended. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for gas too.
Upgrades and some parts increase power or change safety features, and they can void the manufacturer's warranty. Blasters, their parts and equipment, and related items such as printed props (for example cap grenades) are all used at your own risk. Gel blaster laws differ between states and territories and can change, so check the current rules with the Queensland Police Service or the police in your area.
