Title: What The Jack
Description: PolarStar Jack Service/Repair
Breakdown: This absolute workhorse of an AK started being a little weird.

First shot was almost like a jam, second shot rolled out the barrel & the third was ok,
but not what it should have been like.

​Suspecting the nozzle was warped we pulled it right down for an overhaul & replaced the nozzle at the same time.
​What a night and day difference it was, I also had the opportunity to measure it up to design, print & test a new nozzle rubber.
He had issues getting the FPS down after that.

​And in true fashion I have a bad habit of not getting pictures and/or videos once it was back up and running.
