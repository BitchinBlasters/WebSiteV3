Title: VSR HPA Bolt
Description: Electronic controlled HPA Bolt
Breakdown: Here's the first iteration of how I envision an electronically controlled HPA bolt, built for tunability and quietness.
It uses a large Wolverine Inferno solenoid, driven by a Bluetooth microcontroller and a prototype coil drive circuit.

I machined a manifold for the solenoid and air inlet/outlet from solid brass. To minimise the need to modify the blaster itself, I also machined a long solid brass middle section with a well-matched passage size.

To finish, I turned a spigot into the OEM cylinder head/nozzle and soldered the three pieces together. The air inlet lines up with the cylinder slot when the insert is fully tightened into the bolt, followed by a final skim cut on the lathe.

A micro switch on the now-empty trigger assembly gives a very light, short trigger pull while keeping the OEM safety mechanism.

A 3D printed stopper inside the bolt protects the solenoid and wiring from the OEM spring guide pin that holds the bolt in. I made it just thick enough to let the bolt move far enough to cycle the next gel ball in, which keeps the stroke to approx. 12mm for fast cycling.

What's next
The next step is an app for easier tuning and a simpler UI. So far the setup has been very reliable. The biggest issue is the low capacity of the VSR10 magazines, especially now it can be cycles so fast.

Stay tuned, as I'm working on a larger magazine and an adaptor...
